# WhatsApp Broadcast CRM - Advanced Feature Roadmap

This plan outlines the next phase of development to transform the CRM into a market-leading WhatsApp marketing and support platform.

## 1. AI-Powered Automation (The 'Pain Killer')
**User Pain**: Manual replies are time-consuming; broadcast leads often go cold if not handled instantly.
- **AI Agent (GPT-4 Integration)**: Automatically handle common FAQs and qualify leads 24/7.
- **Dynamic Flow Builder**: Visual drag-and-drop interface to create complex automation flows (e.g., "If user clicks button A, send Menu B").
- **Smart Snippets**: AI-suggested quick replies based on conversation context.

## 2. Team Collaboration & Scale
**User Pain**: Single-agent bottlenecks and lack of accountability.
- **Shared Inbox with Assignment**: Assign chats to specific team members or departments (Sales, Support).
- **Internal Private Notes**: Agents can leave notes for each other inside the chat thread that customers can't see.
- **Audit Logs**: Track who sent what message and when.

## 3. E-commerce & Transactional Sync
**User Pain**: Manually checking order status and sending updates.
- **Shopify/WooCommerce Integration**: Automatically send "Order Confirmed" and "Out for Delivery" WhatsApp messages.
- **Interactive Catalogs**: Send product lists directly through WhatsApp with "Add to Cart" buttons.
- **Abandoned Cart Recovery**: Automated WhatsApp nudges for users who left items in their cart.

## 4. Advanced Analytics & Attribution
**User Pain**: Not knowing the ROI of broadcast campaigns.
- **Heatmaps & Click Tracking**: See exactly which buttons in your broadcast were clicked.
- **Conversion Tracking**: Track revenue generated from specific WhatsApp campaigns.
- **Agent Performance Metrics**: Measure response times and resolution rates.

## 5. Rich Interactive Messaging
**User Pain**: Low engagement with plain text messages.
- **List Messages**: Send menus with up to 10 options for users to choose from.
- **Call-to-Action Buttons**: Hardcoded buttons like "Call Us" or "Visit Website" in broadcasts.
- **Carousel Messages**: (Upcoming Meta feature) Multi-card product displays.

---

## Proposed Next Steps (Phase 1: Quick Wins)
1. **Internal Notes**: Implement a simple toggle to add private agent notes in the Inbox.
2. **Interactive Button Support**: Add UI to send "List" and "Button" messages from the Inbox.
3. **Canned Responses**: Create a "Snippets" library for fast one-click replies.
